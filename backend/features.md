Core Utilities

Manual Transaction Entry: To add info about cash transactions.

Search and Filtering: Filter out transactions on the basis of date range or amount range.

Reporting: Monthly or Annual Reports.

Visualizations: Spending Heatmaps.

Net-worth Tracker: Shows record of [total assets - liabilities].

Debt Registry: Simple registry to help track informal debt from friends and family.

Security: Biometric Security options.

Accessibility & Extras

Data Export: Option for downloading transaction history and budget data in CSV or PDF file (useful for tax purposes).

Receipt Scanning: Use phone camera to take photos of physical receipts and attach them to transaction records.

Quick Access: Home screen shortcut to quickly access Manual Transaction Entry.
